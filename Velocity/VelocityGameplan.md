# Velocity Equipment System

## Slots

- Helmet: 1
- Chestplate: 1
- Boots: 1
- Accessories: 3 base, 5 max (+2 slots from the gamepass)

Armour is the main source of power. Accessories give small utility bonuses.

## Power Budget (hard caps)

- Armour total from gear: about 15-20% defensive power at most
- Accessories total from gear: about 5-8% at most
- Cooldown reduction from gear: max 5% (cores already give 10-15%)
- Total cooldown reduction from all sources: soft cap 30%
- Crit chance from gear: max 8% total
- Gear health bonuses can never cancel out Ruby's -20% max health drawback
- Gear bonuses are added together into one pool, then that pool is multiplied with core bonuses
- The same stat can only appear on 2 equipped accessories (stops 5 copies of one item)
- Gear crit chance does nothing until the core allows crits (Ruby from the start, Amethyst and Sapphire at level 8)

## Rarity Names

Item rarity (simple common words):

- Common (grey)
- Good (green)
- Rare (blue)
- Epic (purple)
- Legendary (gold)

Other name ideas if you want to swap them: Basic, Nice, Great, Elite, Max. Or Common, Cool, Rare, Super, Ultra.

Stat rarity uses the same names, so every stat line on an item shows its own colour.

## Item Rarity Effects

Each rarity step adds about 15-20% to base stat values.

- Common: x1.00 stat multiplier, 1 stat line
- Good: x1.15, 2 stat lines
- Rare: x1.30, 2 stat lines
- Epic: x1.50, 3 stat lines
- Legendary: x1.75, 3 stat lines

Accessories use the same rarity multipliers but have fewer lines (Common to Rare get 1 line, Epic and Legendary get 2 lines).

## Stars (built into the item)

- Every item rolls 1 to 5 stars when it drops or is bought
- Stars are permanent and can never be rerolled, upgraded, or changed
- Stars only change how strong the stat values are, never which stats roll
- Star multipliers on top of the rarity multiplier:
  - 1 star: x1.00
  - 2 stars: x1.10
  - 3 stars: x1.20
  - 4 stars: x1.35
  - 5 stars: x1.50
- Star odds on drop:
  - 1 star: 40%
  - 2 stars: 30%
  - 3 stars: 18%
  - 4 stars: 9%
  - 5 stars: 3%
- A 5 star Legendary is the best possible roll, so it is a real chase item
- Final value formula: base value of the stat x rarity multiplier x star multiplier

## Stat Rarity (how good each stat is)

Every stat has its own rarity. Rarer stats are stronger, so they show up less often.

Common stats:
- Max Health
- Health Regen (out of combat only)
- Stamina Regen
- Coin Gain
- Pickup Range

Good stats:
- Energy Regen
- Melee Resist
- Fire Resist
- Magic Resist
- Knockback Resist

Rare stats:
- Damage Reduction
- Movement Speed
- Weapon Damage
- Ability Damage
- Cooldown Reduction (very small, capped)

Epic stats:
- Crit Chance
- Crit Damage
- Parry Window (slightly longer parry timing)

Legendary stats (Legendary items only, 1 line max):
- Tiny Lifesteal
- Revive Boost (faster revive speed for teammates)
- Core Charge (core ultimate charges slightly faster)

## Stat Value Ranges (before multipliers)

Armour (per line, base values):
- Max Health: +3%
- Health Regen: +2%
- Stamina Regen: +2%
- Melee/Fire/Magic Resist: +3%
- Knockback Resist: +4%
- Damage Reduction: +1.5%
- Movement Speed: +1% (heavy armour can have a small speed penalty)
- Weapon Damage: +1.5%
- Ability Damage: +1.5%
- Crit Chance: +1%
- Crit Damage: +3%

Accessories (per line, base values, about a third of armour):
- Max Health: +1%
- Stamina Regen: +1%
- Energy Regen: +1%
- Coin Gain: +2%
- Pickup Range: +5%
- Movement Speed: +0.5%
- Crit Chance: +0.5%
- Weapon/Ability Damage: +0.5%

Quick check on the top end: a 5 star Legendary armour piece gets x1.75 x 1.5 = x2.6 on its base values. That is why the base values stay small.

## Stat Rarity Odds Per Item Rarity

Each stat line rolls its stat rarity using these chances:

Common item:
- Common stat 80%, Good stat 20%

Good item:
- Common stat 60%, Good stat 33%, Rare stat 7%

Rare item:
- Common stat 40%, Good stat 38%, Rare stat 20%, Epic stat 2%

Epic item:
- Common stat 25%, Good stat 35%, Rare stat 30%, Epic stat 10%

Legendary item:
- Common stat 10%, Good stat 30%, Rare stat 40%, Epic stat 18%, Legendary stat 2% (only on the third line)

Once a stat rarity is picked, a random stat from that group is chosen. The same stat can't roll twice on one item.

## Reroll System

Rerolling uses Scrap (gained by dismantling unwanted gear) plus coins.

What you can reroll:
- Reroll One Stat: pick a line and reroll it. Costs the least.
- Reroll All Stats: rerolls every line. Costs more.
- Reroll Values Only: keeps the stats but rerolls the number inside each (a small range, so values stay near what they were). Cheap.

Rules:
- Rarity and stars never change, ever
- A reroll can never give the same stat back on that line
- Lock one stat (costs extra Scrap) to keep it while rerolling the rest. Legendary items can lock up to 2 lines.
- Each reroll on the same item raises the next reroll cost a bit, capped at 3x the base cost
- The cost resets when you swap to a new item
- Dismantle gear to get Scrap. Higher rarity and more stars give more Scrap.
- Reroll odds use the same stat rarity odds as the item's rarity
- Rerolls use Scrap and coins only. Timebux and Robux are never needed, so it stays fair.

## How Gear Is Obtained

- Enemy drops: mostly Common and Good, fewer Rare
- Chests and map events: Good to Epic
- Bosses: Rare to Legendary
- Shop: Common and Good gear only, with random stars
- Each map has its own armour set, so old maps are not trivialised by new gear
- Retroscity hard modes boost drop rarity:
  - Easy: no Epic or Legendary
  - Normal and Hard: Epic possible
  - Impossible: best Legendary odds, and the only place the Retro set can drop at 5 stars with a boosted chance

## Keeping Old Maps Challenging

- Enemies on each map are balanced so that the previous map's full armour set roughly matches the next map's baseline
- A full best-in-slot set of City gear should make City easier, but never trivial
- Impossible mode should still be hard in best Retro gear
- Hard modes scale enemy health and damage so older maps still test good players

### Core Charms

Each core has one charm type. A charm gives a normal small stat, plus a tiny bonus only if you have that core equipped.

- Ruby Charm: +weapon damage, bonus: +1% bloodlust gain
- Sapphire Charm: +energy regen, bonus: +1% ability damage
- Amethyst Charm: +stamina regen, bonus: +1% parry damage
- Diamond Charm: +max health, bonus: +1% healing

Only 1 charm can be equipped at a time.

### Accessory Set Bonuses (optional)

Wear 2 or 3 accessories from the same map for a small bonus:
- 2 City accessories: +1% movement speed
- 3 City accessories: +2% coin gain
- 2 Retro accessories: +1% max health
- 3 Retro accessories: +1% crit chance

## Cosmetic Accessories

Some accessories have almost no stats but look great, to support the accessory slots gamepass. They always roll a single Common stat at the lowest values.

Examples: Party Hat, Traffic Cone Hat, Bunny Ears, Pizza Slice Pin, Paper Crown.

## Quick Balance Check

- Armour: biggest gains, but heavy sets have speed penalties
- Accessories: small gains, up to 5 slots
- Cores stay the main source of identity and power
- Crit, cooldown reduction, and damage stats are rare and capped
- Rarity and stars make gear exciting without being able to break the game
- Rerolling lets players chase good stats without a total reset
